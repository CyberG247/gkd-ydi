About the Organization

Gidan Karan Dawa Youth Development Initiatives (GKD-YDI) is a registered non-governmental organization founded on September 17, 2025, with a mission to empower 10,000 youth across the northeast of Nigeria, starting from Yobe State and specifically Damaturu, by providing education, skills development, and leadership training.

Our vision is to create a future where 10,000 empowered youth, including 5,000 young women, are equipped with the tools and resources for sustainable livelihoods, contributing to the social, economic, and environmental transformation of their communities.

We are committed to improving the quality of life in 50 communities through better access to clean water, sanitation, and hygiene.

Through innovative, community-driven initiatives, we aim to foster a generation of resilient, skilled youth who will lead the region towards sustainable growth, community development, and environmental sustainability.
